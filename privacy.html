Privacy Policy for LumiNino: Baby Tracker

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LumiNino: Baby Tracker ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and name (via Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In).
  • App Data: Information regarding your baby's activities (feeding, sleeping, diapers, etc.) is stored securely.
  • Device & Usage Data: We use analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics) to collect anonymous crash reports and usage data to improve the app.
  • Camera & Photos: The app requires camera/photo library permissions solely for allowing you to set a profile picture for your baby. We do not access or upload your photos for any other purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Services used by the app include:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For analytics, database, and authentication.
  • Google AdMob: For serving ads.
  • RevenueCat: For handling premium subscriptions.

4. Advertising (AdMob) & GDPR/ATT

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising IDs and cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

  • European Union/UK Users: Under GDPR, you will be prompted with a consent form (via Google UMP) to choose whether you accept personalized ads.
  • iOS Users: Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT), you will be asked for permission before we track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for ad targeting.

5. Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely by RevenueCat and the respective app stores (Apple App Store and Google Play). We do not process or store your credit card information.

6. Children's Privacy

Although LumiNino is a baby tracking app, the application is intended for use by parents and adults. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@luminino.com.
